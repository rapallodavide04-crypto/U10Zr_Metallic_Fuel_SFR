# Verified Primary Sources & Figure Mapping

> **Status:** Verified primary repository list (NRC, OSTI/DOE, ANL, INL, PNNL, IAEA, Nature/Frontiers Open Access, Oklo).

## 1. Primary Documents & Technical Scope

### A. NRC Licensing, Qualification & Oklo Industrial Context (2023–2026)
1. **USNRC `NUREG/CR-7305` (Aug 2023)** — *Mother Document*
   - Williams et al., *"Metal Fuel Qualification: Fuel Assessment Using NRC NUREG-2246, 'Fuel Qualification for Advanced Reactors'"* (`ML23214A065`)
   - PDF: https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2321/ML23214A065.pdf
   - Scope: Reference $\text{U-10Zr/HT9}$ design envelope (Sec. 2.3: target burnup, linear power $40\text{--}55\text{ kW/m}$, PICT limit $650\,^\circ\text{C}$, radial strain $\le 2\%$, FCCI and transient eutectic penetration).
2. **PNNL / NRC `PNNL-35642` (2024 / 2026 update)**
   - Wray & Geelhood, *"Degradation and Failure Phenomena of Advanced Reactor Fuel Concepts: Sodium-Cooled Fast Reactor Metallic Fuel"* (`OSTI ID: 3030076`)
   - Scope: Degradation and failure mechanisms of $\text{U-10Zr/HT9}$ (FGR, swelling, FCCI, Run-Beyond-Cladding-Breach, transients).
3. **Crawford & Porter (2024)**
   - *"Applying U.S. metal fuel experience to new fuel designs for fast reactors"*, *Progress in Nuclear Energy* 171 (`INL/JOU-23-75646`)
   - PDF: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2328501
4. **Oklo Aurora & Aurora Fuel Fabrication Facility (A3F) (2024–2026)**
   - NRC Meeting Summary (17 Sep 2025) on Oklo's *"Aurora Fuel Qualification Report"* (referencing `NUREG-2246` and `NUREG/CR-7305`).
   - Oklo / DOE-INL official releases (Nov–Dec 2025) on PDSA approval for **A3F** at INL (electrorefined EBR-II material for first commercial Aurora).
